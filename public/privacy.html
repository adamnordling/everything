1. Data Controller Identity & Contact Details

The Data Controller for Everything Suite is Adam Nordling. Inquiries can be submitted via GitHub Security Advisory or LinkedIn.

2. Local-First Architecture & Zero Tracking

Everything is built as a client-side utility suite. It employs zero tracking cookies, zero remote analytics scripts, and zero advertising beacons. All user state (scratchpad buffers, habit trackers, deadlines) persists solely in the browser's localStorage sandbox and never leaves your device.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

Connection metadata is processed ephemerally on Cloudflare Edge servers strictly under legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for real-time edge routing and DDoS mitigation. Connection logs are discarded immediately.

4. Data Subject Rights

Visitors hold full data subject rights under GDPR Articles 15–22. Because Everything does not store your data on external servers, clearing your browser's site data completely erases all personal state.
